## Contents

- `80-data/public/tos.journal.edn` — EDN quad-log of archived Terms of Use documents,
  each entry carrying `:tos/full-text`, `:tos/source-url`, `:tos/retrieved-at`,
  `:tos/sha256`, `:tos/doc-type`, and a `:tos/supersedes` chain for future revisions.
- `facts.edn` — verified public-registry facts (GLEIF LEI record, ISIN count with
  all 6 ISINs mirrored in full, 3 direct subsidiaries mirrored in full, both
  parent-reporting exceptions, LEI issuer + accreditation, registration authority,
  ISO 20275 legal form), generated by `nbb scripts/verify-facts.cljs --write`.
  Every value carries `:source/url` / `:source/http-status` / `:source/retrieved-at`
  provenance, and check mode (`nbb scripts/verify-facts.cljs`) re-fetches every
  cited source and exits 1 on drift.
- `NOTICE` — copyright/attribution statement for the archived third-party text.
- `blueprint.edn` — machine-readable company identity record, including the public
  inquiry-form URL sourced from the company's own Customer Service page.
